What is malnutrition?

According to the World Health Organization (2020), malnutrition is deficiencies, excesses, or imbalances in a person's energy and/or nutrient consumption. It is divided into two categories.

Undernutrition

A lack of important vitamins and minerals
  • Stunting — low height for age
  • Wasting — low weight for height
  • Underweight — low weight for age
  • Micronutrient deficiencies or insufficiencies

Overweight

Obesity and diet-related noncommunicable diseases
  • Obesity
  • Heart disease
  • Stroke
  • Diabetes
  • Cancer
